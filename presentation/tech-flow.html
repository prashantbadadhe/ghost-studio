🔌 API Endpoints
POST /api/documents/upload Ingest PDF, store bytes
POST /api/documents/{id}/redact Detect PII + apply redaction style
GET /api/documents/{id}/preview/{page} Original page as base64 PNG
GET /api/documents/{id}/redacted-preview/{page} Redacted page as base64 PNG
GET /api/documents/{id}/download-redacted Stream redacted PDF
GET /api/documents/{id}/report JSON audit trail per PII type
POST /api/redact-text Redact plain text, return result
GET /api/policies/ List all compliance policies

GET /api/stats/ Dashboard metrics
⚖️ Compliance Coverage
GLBA Safeguards Rule
16 CFR Part 314 · § 314.4(c)
Bank statements — SSN, account numbers, routing, names, addresses, phone must be protected.
BSA / FinCEN CDD Rule
31 CFR § 1020.220 · § 1010.230
KYC forms — name, DOB, address, govt ID must be safeguarded from unauthorized disclosure.
PCI DSS v4.0
Req. 3.3.1 · Req. 3.5.1
Full PANs must be masked. Last-4-digits style enforces PCI DSS in developer-facing docs.
SOX §§ 302 / 404
15 U.S.C. § 7241, § 7262
Audit reports — tax IDs, employee IDs, account numbers redacted before external sharing.
FCRA
15 U.S.C. § 1681b, § 1681e
Consumer report data may not be re-disclosed. SSN, DOB, credit data protected.
IRS Pub. 1075
Section 9.3 · Exhibit 7
FTI (EINs, SSNs in tax records) must be redacted before sharing with non-authorized parties.
